  <div class="arrival-routes">
    <section class="arrival-route" aria-labelledby="arrival-train">
      <h2 id="arrival-train">By train</h2>
      <p>Plan your journey to <strong>St. Ingbert railway station (Bahnhof)</strong>. D1 is approximately <strong>550 metres away, an eight-minute walk</strong>, at {{ site.workshop.venue_street }}.</p>
      <p>If you arrive at Saarbrücken Hauptbahnhof, plan your onward connection to St. Ingbert before the final walk to D1.</p>
      <a class="text-link" href="https://int.bahn.de/en">Plan a train journey <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
    </section>

    <section class="arrival-route" aria-labelledby="arrival-bus">
      <h2 id="arrival-bus">By bus</h2>
      <p>Use <strong>Rendezvous-Platz</strong>, the central bus station in St. Ingbert, as your destination. From there, D1 is approximately <strong>400 metres away, a six-minute walk</strong>.</p>
      <p>Use Saarfahrplan to find a connection from your hotel or arrival point and check current departure times.</p>
      <a class="text-link" href="https://www.saarfahrplan.de/">Plan local public transport <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
    </section>

    <section class="arrival-route" aria-labelledby="arrival-plane">
      <h2 id="arrival-plane">By plane</h2>
      <p>CISPA lists <strong>Saarbrücken Airport (SCN)</strong> and <strong>Frankfurt Airport (FRA)</strong> as arrival options for the region. Plan onward travel to <strong>St. Ingbert</strong>, then follow the train or bus directions above.</p>
      <p>If taking a taxi, give the driver the workshop address: <strong>{{ site.workshop.venue_street }}, {{ site.workshop.venue_postcode }} {{ site.workshop.venue_city }}</strong>.</p>
    </section>

    <section class="arrival-route" aria-labelledby="arrival-car">
      <h2 id="arrival-car">By car</h2>
      <p>Set your navigation destination to <strong>{{ site.workshop.venue_street }}, {{ site.workshop.venue_postcode }} {{ site.workshop.venue_city }}</strong>.</p>
      <p>CISPA’s D1 guide lists public parking near the <strong>town hall</strong> and on <strong>Wollbachstraße</strong>. Check the signs at your chosen parking area for current fees and time limits.</p>
    </section>
  </div>
